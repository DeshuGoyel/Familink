Security

We can't read your vault.
Not by policy — by mathematics.

Everything is encrypted on your device before it reaches us. We hold scrambled text and nothing else. Even a full breach of our servers reveals nothing about you.

Encryption

AES-256, on your device

Your phone encrypts everything before sending. Our servers only ever see ciphertext — meaningless without a key we never hold.

Key sharing

Shamir's Secret Sharing

Your key splits into fragments — the same protocol that guards critical infrastructure. No one fragment, and no one person, is ever enough.

Architecture

Zero-knowledge by design

Even we can't open your vault. It isn't a promise we make — it's a property the system's design makes impossible to break.
